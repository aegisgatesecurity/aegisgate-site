# Accessibility Statement

**AegisGate Security, LLC**  
**Last Updated: July 29, 2026**

---

## 1. Our Commitment to Accessibility

AegisGate Security, LLC ("AegisGate," "we," "us," "our") is committed to ensuring that our digital products and services are accessible to all individuals, including those with disabilities. We believe that accessible design benefits everyone and that equal access to information and technology is a fundamental right.

We strive to make our website, applications, and documentation perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust for all users, in accordance with the principles outlined in the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C).

Our target conformance level is **WCAG 2.1 Level AA**, and we are actively working toward achieving and maintaining this standard across all of our digital properties.

---

## 2. Current Conformance Status

AegisGate makes its best effort to conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA across all public-facing digital properties, including our corporate website, product documentation, and customer-facing applications.

**Current status:** We are working toward full conformance with WCAG 2.1 Level AA. Our digital properties have not yet undergone a formal third-party accessibility audit. We are committed to conducting a comprehensive accessibility audit by **Q4 2026** and addressing any identified gaps promptly.

Until formal audit results are available, we apply accessibility best practices and perform ongoing internal reviews to identify and remediate barriers to the best of our ability.

---

## 3. Measures Taken

We have implemented the following measures to promote accessibility across our digital properties:

### 3.1 Semantic HTML
We use semantically correct HTML markup to ensure that content is properly structured and can be interpreted by assistive technologies, including screen readers and other adaptive devices.

### 3.2 Keyboard Navigation
All interactive elements on our website and applications are fully operable using keyboard-only navigation. We ensure a logical tab order and visible focus indicators throughout our interfaces.

### 3.3 ARIA Labels
We use Accessible Rich Internet Applications (ARIA) attributes where native HTML semantics are insufficient, providing appropriate labels, roles, and descriptions for custom widgets and dynamic content.

### 3.4 Sufficient Color Contrast
We design our interfaces to meet or exceed the minimum color contrast ratios specified in WCAG 2.1 Success Criteria 1.4.3 (minimum 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text), ensuring that text and interactive elements are distinguishable for users with low vision or color vision deficiencies.

### 3.5 Responsive Design
Our website and applications are designed to be responsive, ensuring usability across a range of devices, screen sizes, and orientations, including when users employ screen magnification or zoom features.

### 3.6 Alt Text for Images
All meaningful images on our digital properties include descriptive alternative text (alt text) that conveys the purpose and content of the image. Decorative images are marked with null alt attributes to be ignored by assistive technologies.

### 3.7 Additional Measures
- Form fields are associated with explicit labels and instructions.
- Error messages are descriptive and programmatically associated with the relevant form fields.
- Content is written in clear, plain language.
- Links and buttons have descriptive text that makes sense out of context.
- Documents available for download are provided in accessible formats wherever possible.
- Video content includes captions where applicable.

## 7. Legal Conformance and Standards

AegisGate is committed to conformance with applicable accessibility laws, regulations, and standards, including but not limited to:

### 7.1 EU Accessibility Act 2025
The European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882), which takes effect on June 28, 2025, establishes accessibility requirements for products and services in the European Union. AegisGate is committed to ensuring that our digital products and services comply with the requirements of this directive as they apply to our operations.

### 7.2 Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA)
The Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) prohibits discrimination against individuals with disabilities in all areas of public life. AegisGate is committed to ensuring that our website and digital services are accessible in accordance with ADA requirements, including the Department of Justice's guidance on web accessibility under Title II and Title III.

### 7.3 Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act
Section 508 (29 U.S.C. § 794d) requires federal agencies and their contractors to ensure that their electronic and information technology is accessible to people with disabilities. AegisGate aligns its accessibility efforts with Section 508 standards, particularly the Section 508 refresh that incorporated WCAG 2.0 Level AA as the baseline standard.

### 7.4 EN 301 549
EN 301 549 is the European standard for accessibility requirements for ICT products and services. AegisGate uses EN 301 549 as a reference framework for ensuring that our products and services meet European accessibility requirements, including those mandated by the EU Accessibility Act.
