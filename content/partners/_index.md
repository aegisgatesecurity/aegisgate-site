# Design Partner Program

AegisGate Security is building the first self-hosted, fail-closed AI security gateway — and we're looking for 3-5 security teams to help us validate it in production.

This is not a sales funnel. It's a structured feedback program with clear scope, clear expectations, and no long-term commitment.

---

## What You Get

- **Free Enterprise tier access** — full platform capabilities, no license fees for the duration of the partnership
- **Direct roadmap input** — your use cases inform what we build next
- **Priority support** — direct email and Slack access to the founder (not a ticket queue)
- **Co-marketing option** — optional case study and joint content (only with explicit permission)
- **Early access to new features** — MCP guardrails, new compliance frameworks, ML detection models before public release

## What We Ask For

- **Production deployment** — run AegisGate against real AI traffic in your environment
- **Monthly feedback call** — 30 minutes, what's working / what's not / what you need
- **Use case sharing** — tell us how your team uses AI tools and what security concerns you have
- **Honest feedback** — bug reports, feature requests, friction points — we need the unfiltered version

## What We Don't Ask For

- No long-term contract
- No exclusivity
- No public reference without your explicit written consent
- No minimum deployment size
- No SLA commitments from us (we're a solo founder — we're honest about response times)

## How It Works

1. **Intro call (20 min)** — we discuss your AI security needs and whether AegisGate is a fit
2. **Deployment setup** — we help you deploy the platform in your environment (Docker, Helm, or bare metal)
3. **Configuration** — we tune detection patterns and compliance frameworks to your use case
4. **Production run** — you use it against real traffic; we check in weekly for the first two weeks
5. **Monthly feedback** — 30-min call, structured feedback, roadmap discussion
6. **Ongoing** — month-to-month, cancel anytime, no questions asked

## Program Constraints

Being transparent about our capacity:

- **Maximum 5 partners** — we're a solo founder, and we can't support more than that
- **No custom feature guarantees** — we welcome roadmap input but can't promise specific features by specific dates
- **No 24/7 support** — email and Slack access, best-effort response within 1 business day
- **No production SLA** — AegisGate is production-ready but this is a design partnership, not a vendor relationship
