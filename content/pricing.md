### Why Open Source?

We believe security tools should be transparent, auditable, and community-driven. Every line of detection logic is public. Every pattern is reviewable. Every claim is verifiable.

That's why ~830,000 lines of Go and 16,000+ tests are on GitHub — not behind a paywall.

Enterprise engagements simply add support, compliance mapping, and integration work on top of the same open-source core.
